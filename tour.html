LJSportMind

How it works

Find your place in the sports industry.

Discover careers that fit your experience, identify your skill gaps, and build your path forward.

LJSportMind is built for college students and early-career professionals who want to work in sports. It's free to use, by LJSOCCER (TikTok @ljsoccer01).

Build my Sports Passport

1Build your Sports Passport

Your Sports Passport is your profile and evidence record. You add your name and location, current role (player, former player, coach, student, content creator, fan or other), skills, interests, career goal, education and achievements, one per line. You can also import a resume (PDF or text). It's read in your browser to suggest skills and is never uploaded. A completeness bar shows what to add next. The Passport is saved on your device, and you can optionally lock it with Face ID, a fingerprint, or a favorite-player answer.

2Discover roles that fit you

LJSportMind compares your Passport with eight sports career paths: Sports Content Creator, Team Video / Media Analyst, Sports Journalist / Broadcaster, Youth or Club Coach, Scouting / Analytics, Marketing & Sponsorship, Event Operations and Front Office / Team Operations. It shows your top matches. Each match includes:

  • Match strength: Strong, Good or Possible, plus a plain count such as "Covers 4 of 6 skills this role typically uses". There are no percentage scores: matches are guidance, not a validated assessment.
  • Why it fits, in plain language.
  • Evidence: where each matching skill comes from, either listed in your Passport, quoted from one of your achievements, or from your career goal.
  • Missing: the typical skills you haven't shown yet.
  • Next steps: concrete actions for that role, plus job-search links to Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, TeamWork Online and WorkInSports.

3Close your skill gaps

Skills that open the most doors

A chart of the missing skills that come up most across your top roles, for example "Writing: missing in 3 of your top 4 roles". Building one of them improves several matches at once.

Your AI Coach

One place that analyses and coaches. It gives a quick read of your closest match, your strengths, the gap that matters most and your progress since you first saved, then your next steps. When the AI service is available, it writes a full analysis: an overall read, strengths with evidence, gaps, and a 7-day plan. You can then chat with it about your path. It only uses facts from your Passport and doesn't invent jobs, companies or statistics.

4Check a real opportunity

Paste a job or internship posting. LJSportMind lists the skills it mentions, which you match ("You match 3 of 5 skills this posting mentions"), what's missing and whether it asks for a degree. It's based on keywords, so treat it as a guide, not a hiring prediction. Then send it to your AI Coach with "Ask my coach about this".

Extras, separate from career discovery

Creator Studio

For content creators: enter a game and the key moment to get a hook, video plan, caption with hashtags, comment starter and a timed voiceover script. Captions carry "Made with LJSportMind by @ljsoccer01".

Sports Lab (experimental)

Body-tracking soccer mini-games (Penalty Kick, Free Kick, Long-Range Assist, Keeper Reflex, Header Timing, Keepy-Uppy) and Movement Memory, a one-minute camera check of posture, squats and balance. Pose tracking runs on your device and video is never uploaded. It's a guide, not medical advice.
